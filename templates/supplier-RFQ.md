# 供应商询价单 RFQ（可复制发送）

Subject: Request for quotation – [SKU / product specification]

Hello [Name],

We are evaluating a small pilot order for [product], to be sold in [target country]. Please provide a written quote covering:

1. Exact material, dimensions, color, packaging and labeling.
2. Minimum order quantity (MOQ) and price tiers for 10/100/500 units.
3. Sample cost, sample lead time and production lead time.
4. Incoterm, origin, proposed shipping options and estimated freight cost.
5. Product testing/certifications available and actual report numbers.
6. Defective-item policy, refund/repair process and responsibility for delays.
7. Payment terms, accepted currency and whether a written invoice is available.

Please attach a current specification sheet and photos of the **actual** product.

Thanks,
[Your business name]

---

内部核验：不要把 AI 翻译的认证描述直接当作实际证书；比较至少两家供应商。记录报价日期和币种，不把预计价格写为已成交成本。
